Potato Leek Soup with Garlic Sourdough

Velvety potato and leek soup finished with a swirl of cream, served with crisp garlic-butter sourdough for dunking.

Prep time: 15 minutes. Cook time: 40 minutes. Total: about 55 minutes. Serves 4.

    Ingredients

  • 3 large leeks (white and light green parts), sliced and washed
  • 600 grams floury potatoes (Maris Piper or Russet), peeled and cubed
  • 30 grams unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, 2 sliced and 1 grated
  • 900 milliliters hot vegetable stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 100 milliliters single or light cream
  • 1 teaspoons salt
  • 0.5 teaspoons white or black pepper
  • 1 pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 4 thick slices sourdough bread
  • 40 grams soft butter, for the garlic bread
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives or parsley
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

    Process

  1. Prep the leeks: Trim the dark green tops off the 3 large leeks (white and light green parts), sliced and washed, halve lengthways, slice and rinse well in a bowl of cold water to remove grit. Drain thoroughly.
  2. Sweat the leeks: Melt 30 grams unsalted butter with the 1 tablespoons olive oil in a large heavy pot over medium-low heat. Add the leeks and a pinch of 1 teaspoons salt. Cook gently, stirring often, until completely soft but not browned. Add the sliced garlic from 3 garlic cloves, 2 sliced and 1 grated for the last minute.
  3. Simmer the soup: Add the 600 grams floury potatoes (Maris Piper or Russet), peeled and cubed, 900 milliliters hot vegetable stock, 2 bay leaves and 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, partially covered, until the potatoes are very tender and fall apart when pressed.
  4. Make the garlic sourdough: Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan / 400°F). Mix 40 grams soft butter, for the garlic bread with the grated garlic from 3 garlic cloves, 2 sliced and 1 grated and a pinch of salt. Spread over 4 thick slices sourdough bread and bake on a tray until golden and crisp at the edges.
  5. Blend until smooth: Remove the bay leaves. Blend the soup with a stick blender (or in batches in a countertop blender) until completely smooth. Return to low heat.
  6. Add cream and season: Stir in 100 milliliters single or light cream, 0.5 teaspoons white or black pepper, 1 pinch of ground nutmeg and 1 tsp lemon juice. Warm through gently without boiling. Taste and adjust the salt, and thin with a splash of stock or water if too thick.
  7. Serve: Ladle into warm bowls, add a swirl of cream, scatter with 2 tbsp chopped chives or parsley and serve with the hot garlic sourdough for dunking.

Notes

Tips: Wash the leeks well, as dirt hides between the layers. Cook them slowly without browning for the sweetest, silkiest flavour. Floury potatoes blend creamier than waxy ones, and avoid over-blending, which can make the soup gluey.

Variations: top with crispy sourdough croutons instead of the garlic toast, or add a spoonful of crème fraîche, crumbled blue cheese or crispy fried leek strands. For vegan, use olive oil, plant-based cream and dairy-free butter. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3 days and freeze well for up to 3 months (add the cream when reheating).
